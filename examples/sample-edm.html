Communique Summit 2025

IT'S A WRAP!

The 4th edition of the Communique Summit was held on 31st August 2025 at Taj Palace, New Delhi. With the theme of “India ka Future — 10 Saal Pragati ke Naam”, the summit was also a celebration of our 10th anniversary — bringing together industry experts, policy makers, and worldwide leaders.

The summit was livestreamed on Communique’s website and partner channels including Economic Times, Mint, Money Control, and News18. Through a combination of print media, livestreaming, and social media amplification, the summit clocked an audience reach of 10.7 MM.
